The [Rocket Propulsion Laboratory](https://rocketproplab.org) (RPL) is UC San Diego's student organization for designing and building rockets and propulsion systems.

## Daedalus Project Lead (Fall 2026 – present)

Daedalus is RPL's onboarding project. I lead a cohort of about 65 students in teams of 4–5 who design, build and launch G-class rockets. I help develop the engineering curriculum, mentor 2–3 teams directly across CAD, 3D printing, analysis, avionics and launch operations, and coordinate teams toward flight testing.

## Co-lead Chief Engineer, Analysis team (Fall 2025 – Spring 2026)

I ran CFD, FEA and thermal simulations in Ansys to test rocket components under realistic flight loads, and worked with the structures, avionics and propulsion subteams to validate their designs. I also managed the team's simulation workflow and trained newer members. During this time I earned my [Level 1 certification](/projects/l1-rocket/) and helped 11 members earn theirs.

## Daedalus team member (Fall 2023 – Spring 2024)

My first year: I helped design, build and launch a G-class rocket, designing parts in SolidWorks and OpenRocket, 3D printing them, and assisting with avionics and soldering.
