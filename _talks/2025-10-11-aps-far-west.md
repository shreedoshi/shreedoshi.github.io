With Jérôme Maire, Shelley A. Wright and the Panoramic SETI team.

This poster describes the first version of the distortion-map method, applied to imaging data from a Panoramic SETI telescope at Lick Observatory taken on November 18, 2022. After removing the detector's static noise pattern, I identified stars by comparison with the Hipparcos catalog, centroided them, and converted their positions to right ascension and declination with a gnomonic projection. Linear interpolation of the differences between predicted and measured positions produced distortion maps for single and combined datasets.

The astrometric solution used by Panoramic SETI at the time could introduce errors of about ±0.15°, around half a pixel, across the 10° × 10° field. The distortion maps correct these errors, so detected optical transients can be located more accurately. The [project page](/research/panoseti-astrometry/) shows the later, automated version of the pipeline.
