--- layout: modern title: "Outreach & mentoring" summary: "Building a more welcoming physics department, and helping other students get their first rockets off the ground." permalink: /outreach/ ---

  • Advocating for and Representing Minority Students (ARMS)

    Society of Physics Students, UC San Diego, 2025 – present

    ARMS gives minority and underrepresented students a voice on diversity in the Physics Department. I organized a town hall that brought faculty and 30 students together to talk openly about academics, professional support and department climate, and organized two Voices in Physics seminars where physicists shared their paths into the field.

  • Daedalus Project Lead

    Rocket Propulsion Laboratory, UC San Diego, Fall 2026 – present

    Daedalus is RPL's onboarding project: about 65 new members, in teams of 4–5, design, build and launch G-class rockets. I help shape the curriculum and mentor 2–3 teams directly through CAD, 3D printing, analysis, avionics and launch operations.

  • Level 1 certification support

    Rocket Propulsion Laboratory, UC San Diego, 2025 – 2026

    After earning my own Level 1 certification, I wrote certification documentation for the club, coordinated bulk motor purchases, and guided 11 members through earning theirs.

  • Space Is Calling

    Birch Aquarium, Scripps Institution of Oceanography, May 2026

    Represented RPL at a public event featuring a live call with NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Chris Williams aboard the International Space Station, showing our rockets and talking with visitors about undergraduate rocketry.

  • Instructional Assistant

    Department of Physics, UC San Diego, 2024 – present

    Grade lab reports and practical exams for physics courses, with written feedback aimed at helping students improve their next report.

  • Dubai Astronomy Group

    Member, 2019 – 2023

    Stargazing nights, meteor shower observations and public outreach sessions with experienced amateur astronomers. This is where my interest in astrophysics and instrumentation started.
