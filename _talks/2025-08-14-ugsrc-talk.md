A contributed talk introducing Panoramic SETI, an all-sky optical SETI observatory with a field of view of more than 2,000 square degrees, and why its wide-field telescopes need astrometric distortion maps. The talk walks through how I measure the distortion and build the maps. See the [project page](/research/panoseti-astrometry/) for the current pipeline.
