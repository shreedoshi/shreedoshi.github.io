At Astrotech at UC Berkeley, my team designed and built a spectrograph aimed at two targets: identifying aurorae on Jupiter and detecting kilonovae.

## My role

As electronics lead, I programmed a motorized stage that moves the detector so the spectrograph can cover a wider wavelength range than a fixed detector allows. I then integrated the stage with the calibration, optics, detector and software components built by the other team members, and we presented the finished instrument to researchers and other participants.
