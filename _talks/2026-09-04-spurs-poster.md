With Jérôme Maire, Shelley A. Wright, Alyssa Johnson and the Panoramic SETI team.

This poster presents an automated pipeline that characterizes optical distortion across PANOSETI's 10° × 10° field of view. It cleans raw imaging-mode data, centroids stars, matches them to VizieR catalog positions, and interpolates the residuals into distortion maps in right ascension and declination. Astrometric errors are about ±0.3°, roughly one detector pixel. Read more on the [project page](/research/panoseti-astrometry/).
