I'm a physics student at UC San Diego (B.S. Physics, specialization in astrophysics, minor in mathematics, class of 2027), working where astrophysics meets instrumentation.

In [Prof. Shelley Wright's](https://oirlab.ucsd.edu/) Optical and Infrared Laboratory, I develop an automated pipeline that measures and corrects optical distortion for [Panoramic SETI](/research/panoseti-astrometry/), a wide-field observatory at Palomar searching for nanosecond-to-second optical transients. I'm a co-author on two Panoramic SETI papers in *Proc. SPIE* (2026).

Outside the lab I design, simulate and fly rockets with UC San Diego's [Rocket Propulsion Laboratory](/projects/rpl/), where I lead the new-member Daedalus program. My independent project combines the two: a [ground-based optical system to track rockets](/projects/rocket-tracking/) in flight.

I'm applying to graduate programs in astronomy and astrophysics, with a focus on instrumentation. Reach me at [s2doshi@ucsd.edu](mailto:s2doshi@ucsd.edu).
