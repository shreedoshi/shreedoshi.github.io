I'm a physics student at UC San Diego (B.S. Physics, specialization in astrophysics, minor in mathematics, class of 2027), working on astronomical observations and instrumentation.

In the Optical and Infrared Laboratory ([OIRLab](https://oirlab.ucsd.edu/)) at UCSD, I worked on developing an automated pipeline that measures and corrects optical distortion for [Panoramic SETI](/research/panoseti-astrometry/), a wide-field observatory at Palomar searching for nanosecond-to-second optical transients. Currently I am planning a search for optical counterparts of Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs).

Outside the lab I design, simulate and fly rockets with UC San Diego's [Rocket Propulsion Laboratory](/projects/rpl/) (RPL), where I am co-lead of Mission Control. My independent project is a [ground-based optical system to track rockets](/projects/rocket-tracking/) in flight.

I'm applying to graduate programs in astronomy and astrophysics, with a focus on instrumentation.
